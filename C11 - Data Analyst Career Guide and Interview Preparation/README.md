# Course 11: Data Analyst Career Guide and Interview Preparation

## 📖 Overview

This repository contains my notes, hands-on labs, interview preparation resources, assignments, and career development materials completed as part of **Course 11 – Data Analyst Career Guide and Interview Preparation** from the **IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate**.

The course focuses on preparing aspiring data analysts for the job market by covering career planning, resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, networking strategies, job search resources, interview preparation, technical skill review, and professional communication. It also includes hands-on SQL and Python exercises to reinforce core analytical skills required for technical interviews.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this course, I can:

- Identify career opportunities in data analytics
- Build a professional resume and cover letter
- Optimize my LinkedIn profile
- Research companies and industries effectively
- Evaluate job listings and identify required skills
- Develop a strategic job search plan
- Prepare for behavioral and technical interviews
- Refresh essential SQL and Python skills
- Communicate professionally during the hiring process
- Build a long-term career development plan
